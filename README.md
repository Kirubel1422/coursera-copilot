Coursera Autopilot is a Chrome extension that streamlines working through Coursera courses. A compact popup lets you flip it on, pick a playback speed from 1× to 16× (with quick presets), and toggle features individually: auto-advancing to the next item when a video ends, "smart skip" to jump ahead after a short preview, and automatic dismissal of modal popups. Settings sync across your browser and apply instantly to any open Coursera tab without a refresh.
